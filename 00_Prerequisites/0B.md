# Group 0B: PyTorch Tensors and Autograd

## Group Overview

Tensors with the same shape may have different layouts, and they may also share the same storage; even when the forward result is correct, gradients may still be cut off or accidentally accumulated. This group starts from tensor attributes and indexing, then traces the backward pass and how gradients are saved, cleared, and disabled.

## Learning Path

### Recommended Order

Read in the order 05 → 06 → 07 → 08. For shape or indexing errors, check 05 and 06 first; for missing gradients, duplicate accumulation, or freezing anomalies, check 07 and 08 first.
