# Group 1C: Communication, Parallelism and Fault Tolerance

This group explains the capacity gains, communication costs, and recovery requirements brought by multi-device splitting.

## Group Goal

In this group, learners answer three questions: which objects need to be split, what data is exchanged at each step, and how to recover after a system interruption. Topologies and collective communication give the exchange conditions, and the training-state ledger gives the capacity conditions; after the two streams of information converge, parallel candidates are filtered, and the communication critical path and recovery cost are examined separately. It is recommended to first complete the scale ledger of [1A](./1A.md) and the single-device resource model of [1B](./1B.md).
