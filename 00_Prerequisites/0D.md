# Group 0D: Transformer Block Dataflow and Component Basics

## Group Overview

A Transformer Block updates the hidden state through two branches, Attention and MLP, and both residual additions require shape alignment. This group first looks at the complete Block, then separately observes how activations, normalization, and Attention compute their outputs.

![0D going from the Block overview to activation, normalization, and Attention](../docs/public/00_Prerequisites/0d_training_model_map.svg)
