# Group 2.10: Project Validation and Engineering Closure

This group carries integrated projects, performance comparisons, and training analysis, and is the project closure layer of Part 2. It no longer expands the principle of a single method; instead, it condenses the capabilities of the preceding groups into engineering validation entries that are comparable, reproducible, and actionable.

## Group Overview

This group puts the mechanisms of the preceding groups back into real workloads to form six project chains: post-training and alignment, inference optimization and deployment, performance optimization, LLM architecture evolution, quantization and compression, and communication and parallelism. Each page has only one primary owner; other topics only reuse its results and links.
