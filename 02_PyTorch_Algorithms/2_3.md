# Group 2.3: Backpropagation and Training Control

This group unfolds along a single training update: first trace the local gradients of the loss and Attention, then determine the update boundaries of gradient accumulation, mixed precision, the optimizer, and learning-rate scheduling, and finally compare activation recomputation, movement, and training-state residency. Continual pretraining is an application branch of parameter-update dynamics, not a mandatory step for every training task.

![The relationship among the three paths of backward signal, effective update, and training state](../docs/public/02_PyTorch_Algorithms/2_3_training_control_learning_path.svg)

### In-Group Path

- Backward signal: [P0 · 07](../00_Prerequisites/07_PyTorch_Autograd_and_Backward.ipynb) → [09](./09_Activation_and_Loss_Backward.ipynb) → [10](./10_Attention_Backward_and_Custom_Autograd.ipynb).
- Effective update: [11](./11_Gradient_Accumulation.ipynb) → [TRAIN-MIXED-PRECISION-BACKWARD](./TRAIN-MIXED-PRECISION-BACKWARD_Mixed_Precision_Backward.ipynb) → [12](./12_Optimizer_Updates_and_Learning_Rate_Scheduling.ipynb) → [55](./55_Training_Update_Dynamics.ipynb). Mixed precision first determines whether the current gradients are finite and whether the parameters actually update, and the scheduler then decides whether to advance.
- Training state: [19](./19_Activation_Checkpointing.ipynb) and [42](./42_Activation_Offload.ipynb) are two parallel strategies of recomputation and movement, and they are then compared uniformly in [43](./43_Unified_Training_Memory_Ledger.ipynb) for residency, peak, and time cost.
- Application exit: when full-sequence domain continued training is needed, enter [56](./56_Continual_Pretraining_and_Retention.ipynb) from 55; when supervised fine-tuning and parameter adaptation are needed, enter [2.4](./2_4.md).

For the cross-cutting route, see the [backpropagation and training mechanics topic](../topic_discussion/backpropagation_training_mechanism/intro.md).
